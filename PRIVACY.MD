# Privacy Policy for Kura

**Effective Date:** September 3, 2026

Kura ("the App") is an offline-first, locally encrypted digital wallet and pass management tool. We believe your personal data belongs solely to you.

## Information Collection and Use
Kura does not collect, track, store, transmit, or share any personal information with external servers or third parties. 

- **Local Storage & Encryption:** All payment cards, passes, identities, and metadata added to the app are stored exclusively on your device within an encrypted SQLCipher database. 
- **Biometrics & Authentication:** Biometric data (fingerprint or facial recognition) is handled entirely by the Android operating system. Kura never accesses or stores your raw biometric credentials.
- **External Files:** Files imported into the app (such as `.pkpass` files) remain stored locally on your device in secure sandbox storage.

## Third-Party Services
The App contains no third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytics frameworks.

## Data Deletion
Since all data resides locally on your device, deleting individual items within the app or uninstalling the App permanently removes all stored data and keys.
