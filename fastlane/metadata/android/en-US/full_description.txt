Privacy first pass wallet.

Keep your cards, tickets and identity documents together in three separate sections: Cards, Passes and Identity. Kura works offline, without accounts, advertising or telemetry, and does not request Internet access.

Organize your wallet
- Import .pkpass files and .pkpasses bundles.
- Add items manually with category-specific fields, custom fields and attachments.
- Store logos and front/back images, and scan or import barcodes.
- Find items with search, categories, favorites, sorting and archive.
- Open barcodes full screen for scanning.

Keep control of your data
- Unlock with supported biometrics or your device credential.
- Encrypted databases and media with automatic vault locking.
- Password-encrypted backups and optional automatic backups to a folder you choose.
- Light and dark themes, with adjustable layouts and navigation.

Kura stores card references and barcodes. It does not make NFC payments. Imported pass issuer signatures are not verified. Opening links, sharing passes or viewing exported attachments hands data to another app.

Upgrading from Kura 1.x: create and keep a backup before updating. Restore that backup in Kura 2.0; old private app storage is not migrated automatically. If an older backup omitted images, Kura warns before restoring the available records and media.

Requires Android 7.0 or later, a secure device lock and supported hardware-backed key storage.
