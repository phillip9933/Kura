Kura 2.0 rebuilds the wallet in native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.
- Separate Cards, Passes and Identity sections.
- Favorites, sorting, custom fields, images and attachments.
- Biometric or device-credential unlock and encrypted backups.
- Improved recovery when older backups contain missing image references.
Before upgrading from 1.x, create and keep a backup, then restore it in 2.0. Migration is not automatic.
